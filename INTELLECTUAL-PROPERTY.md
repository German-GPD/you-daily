This document is an internal project record. It is intended to help keep the development history and ownership questions organized. It is not a patent application, trademark registration, legal opinion, or proof that any particular idea is legally exclusive.

## Project description

You Daily is a concept for a personalized AI newspaper. The proposed product can use information from sources deliberately connected by a user, analyze and prioritize that information, prepare a readable newspaper-style edition, and optionally deliver it through a compact printer on a schedule.

The product direction includes:
- User-authorized connections to selected information sources
- Personal relevance and topic preferences
- AI-assisted selection, summarization and contextualization
- Newspaper-style editorial design and print formatting
- Optional scheduled physical delivery

This is a high-level description, not a disclosure of confidential implementation details.

## Ownership and contributions

Before accepting outside contributions, clarify in writing:
- Who owns the contributed code, designs, text and other materials
- Whether the contributor grants a license or assigns rights
- Whether third-party or open-source components are included
- Whether confidentiality obligations apply

Do not assume that paying for work automatically transfers all intellectual-property rights. Use written agreements appropriate to the jurisdiction.

## Potential protection areas

Different parts of a product may require different approaches:

- **Copyright:** may protect original code, text, graphics and other creative expression. It generally does not give exclusive ownership of an abstract idea or business concept.
- **Trademark:** may protect a distinctive product name or logo in relevant markets after appropriate clearance and registration. The availability of “You Daily” has not been verified.
- **Patent:** may be relevant only if there is a patentable technical invention and the legal requirements are met. A broad product idea or ordinary combination of known features is not automatically patentable.
- **Trade secrets:** confidential technical, commercial or operational information may be protected when reasonable steps are taken to keep it secret.

## Before public disclosure

Keep this repository private while evaluating whether any technical details could be relevant to a patent filing. Public disclosure before filing can affect patent rights in some countries. Consult a qualified patent professional before publishing enabling technical details or presenting the invention publicly.

Also conduct a proper trademark search before investing heavily in the name, logo, domain or marketing.

## Practical safeguards

- Keep credentials, API tokens, private user data and signing keys out of Git.
- Use private repositories and restricted access for confidential work.
- Enable strong account security and review collaborator permissions.
- Keep backups of important source files and project records.
- Record third-party assets and their licenses.
- Use written confidentiality and IP agreements where appropriate.
